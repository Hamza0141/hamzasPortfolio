The business needed a centralized software system to manage employees, customers, vehicles, services, and orders instead of relying on fragmented manual processes.

02

Solution

I built a full-stack management platform that supports employee administration, customer records, vehicle information, service listings, order creation, and order tracking.

03

Impact

The platform improved operational visibility, streamlined service workflows, and contributed to measurable business growth in sales and profit.

Key Features

Employee Management

Allows the business to add, update, view, and manage employee records through an internal dashboard.

Customer Management

Provides tools to register customers, manage customer details, and maintain organized service-related records.

Vehicle Registration

Supports customer vehicle information management, helping the business keep accurate and up-to-date vehicle records.

Order Creation and Tracking

Enables staff to create service orders, update order details, and track service progress through the system.

Service Management

Organizes available services so employees can manage service offerings and connect them to customer orders.

Secure Authentication

Uses JWT-based authentication to protect employee access, internal routes, and business management features.

Technology Stack

Frontend

React.js, Material UI, FontAwesome, HTML5, CSS3

Backend

Node.js, Express.js, REST APIs

Database

MySQL relational database for employees, customers, vehicles, services, and orders.

Authentication

JWT authentication for secure employee login, authorization, and protected routes.

Deployment

Ubuntu remote server, Nginx reverse proxy, and production domain configuration.

Hosting

Hostinger VPS hosting for live deployment and business system availability.
